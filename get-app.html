Get Started

Join your team on Greenfly

Have an invite code?

  1. 1

    Download the Greenfly app

    Free on iPhone and Android.

  2. 2

    Open it and enter your invite code

    Your code comes from the team or brand that invited you — usually in your invitation email.

  3. 3

    You're in

    Start receiving, creating, and sharing the content that features you.

Got an email?

  1. 1

    Open the email and tap Login

    It opens in your web browser.

  2. 2

    Finish setting up

    Confirm your details and choose a new password.

  3. 3

    You're set

    Sign in anytime — in the app or at portal.greenfly.com.

Start from the email link. Opening the app and signing in before you've finished this step returns a login error.
